  • Opt-in method (explicit): 1) Website form at nqai.llc with checkbox "I agree to receive text messages from NqAI LLC at the number provided, for appointment confirmations, job updates, and support. Msg rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help." 2) Verbal in-person at business: staff asks and logs consent. 3) Customer initiates text to our Super Phone number.

  • Message frequency: Variable, typically 1-4 messages per transaction/job, not more than 10 per month per customer unless ongoing service.
  • Sample messages (actual):
